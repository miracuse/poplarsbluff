# Builder's Blog

{: .summary}
> Short primers on how to modify your game.

### What Is This?

The articles in this section are hands-on guides to changing Neverwinter's core mechanics. Easier tutorials are near the top, while harder tutorials are near the bottom.

### Why Is This?

Many of the original guides have been lost to time. The posts on this site are an attempt to preserve and revive that know-how via simple tutorials.

The articles guide a reader through the process of making simple changes to their local game. They also set the reader up to make larger changes on their own.
