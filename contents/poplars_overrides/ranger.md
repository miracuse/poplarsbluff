**Hit die**: d10

**Proficiencies**: armor (light, medium), shields, weapons (martial, simple)

**Skill points**: 4 + int modifier ( (4 + int modifier) * 4 at 1st level) 

**BaB Progression**: +1 / level (High)

**Bonus Feat List**: ambidexterity, two weapon fighting, improved two weapon fighting, point blank shot, rapid shot, rapid reload, favored enemy, weapon finesse

**Skill List**: animal empathy, concentration, craft armor, craft trap, craft weapon, *disable trap*, discipline, heal, hide, listen, lore, move silently, parry, ride, search, set trap, spot, *tumble*

**Spellcasting**: spontaneous, WIS-based, spell list below

**Special**: animal companion options include wizard/sorcerer familiars
