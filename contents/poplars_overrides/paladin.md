**Hit die**: d10

**Proficiencies**: armor (light, medium, heavy), shields, weapons (simple, martial)

**Skill points**: 2 + int modifier ( (2 + int modifier) * 4 at 1st level) 

**BaB Progression**: +1 / level (High)

**Skill List**: concentration, craft armor, craft trap, craft weapon, discipline, heal, lore, parry, persuade, ride, taunt

**Spellcasting**: charisma-based, spontaneous, modified spell list

**Bonus Feats**: aura of courage, divine grace, divine might, divine shield, divine health, vermin turning, elemental turning, construct turning, outsider turning, empowered turning, extra turning, extra smiting, smite evil

**Special**: alignment options now include lawful neutral, neutral good, and true neutral
