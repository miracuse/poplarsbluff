# Playtest Log - SoU

### Paladin - Half Elf

* I've never actually played a Paladin for SoU.
  * Holy cow, the magic armor kind of blows the power curve.

* The undead Monk is really strong.
  * Was that a result of my changes, or was he just that way to begin with?
  * The magic sword is also rather overpowered.

* Kel'Garas seems to have lost access to spellcasting.
  * Might need to rework the Cleric class to avoid underpowering Cleric enemies.
  * Could duplicate the existing class and spell list, then lock it down so that players can only use the new version.

* I goofed the *Divine Might* and *Divine Shield* feats.
  * Need to swap those to bonus feats, or normal feats.

* *Darkfire* is a little strong.
  * I should drop the caster level down to halves.
  * Think I saw a Discord post about setting that value to 0.5 for that.

---

I'd count this playthrough as a success. Definitely need to look into Cleric NPC's potentially losing their abilities, but the overall experience was positive.
