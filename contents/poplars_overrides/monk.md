**Hit die**: d12

**Proficiencies**: no armor, weapons (monk, simple)

**Skill points**: 4 + int modifier ( (4 + int modifier) * 4 at 1st level) 

**BaB Progression**: +1 / level (High)

**Skill List**: concentration, craft armor, craft trap, craft weapon, discipline, heal, hide, listen, lore, move silently, parry, persuade, tumble

**Spellcasting**: none

**Special**: monk weapon proficiencies have changed

{: .summary}
> The following weapons can be used with *Flurry of Blows*. Weapons marked with an asterisk are compatible, but require additional proficiencies to use.

The Revised Monk is modestly different from their 3E counterpart.<br><br>

The new Monk is a high speed, high health combatant that specializes in Simple weapons. They have similar statistics to a Barbarian (hit die, BaB, skill points).<br><br>

In exchange for higher base stats, the Revised Monk no longer receives many of their class-specific abilities. They can, however, take them as standard feats at the appropriate level. Monks also gain access to an improved version of Flurry of Blows which is compatible with weapons besides the kama.
