**Skill List**: concentration, craft armor, craft trap, craft weapon, discipline, heal, *intimidate*, lore, parry, persuade, ride, taunt

**Bonus Feats**: aura of courage, divine grace, divine might, divine shield, divine health, vermin turning, elemental turning, construct turning, outsider turning, empowered turning, extra turning, extra smiting, smite evil

**Special**: Alignment options now include lawful neutral, neutral good, and true neutral.
